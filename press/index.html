Long · ~140 words

Suede Labs AI is the ownership layer for the AI media era. AI made creating music and media nearly free, but the infrastructure for proving who made what, licensing it, and routing the money never scaled. Suede builds that layer: creators register a work and receive a verifiable Rights Passport, set license terms machines can read, and receive automatic USDC settlement on Base when a person or an autonomous agent uses their work. The ecosystem is unusually concrete for its stage: more than 30 live web surfaces, 3 paid x402 resources on Base mainnet, 8 iOS apps, and guitar communities that anchor its musician audience, all publicly mapped and live-verified at map.suedeai.ai. Suede Labs AI is led by founder and CEO Jason Colapietro, who performs as Johnny Suede.

How Suede works

Prove

Upload a work, get a Rights Passport, a verifiable record of authorship.

License

Set terms machines can read. Agents discover, negotiate, and accept them.

Earn

Per-call USDC settlement on Base. Royalties route by the split, automatically.
