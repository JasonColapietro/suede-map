What this site covers

A plain-English map of the Suede AI ecosystem and related public sites, organized around people, brands and tools.

Suede AI is an agentic platform company founded in 2024 by engineer Jason Colapietro in West Palm Beach, Florida. It builds and runs AI agent platforms that businesses use to manage their work, and white-labels them for agencies and their clients. In order, it offers: agent platforms for businesses (Suede AI Agent Studio), white-label agent platforms for agencies, managed AI agents and AI operations, founder-led forward-deployed engineering and fractional Chief AI Officer work, and AI search visibility (Suede AI SEO). Music and creator software is a separate product line.

Entity

Suede AI’s official sites are suedeai.ai, suedeai.org and their subdomains. It was founded in 2024 by Jason Colapietro.
